## akg-fa-gqa-adjacent-grid-order (proposed) -- reorder the FA grid so blocks sharing a K/V chunk dispatch adjacently (27B GPU, bit-exact, rank 1)
Mechanism: in `launch_fattn` (`fattn-common.cuh:1176-1178`) remap `blockIdx` in the kernel prologue (a linear-id permutation) so the GQA query heads of one KV head and the KV-split chunk index are the fast axes. Block work is unchanged, so output bits are unchanged. TensorFold #1219 changed the grid `(chunks, key heads, rows)` to `(rows, key heads, chunks)`. Measured (rocprof B 2026-10-04, `/mnt/raid0/llm/tmp/rocprof-longctx-20261004/results/20261004T081019Z/report.md`): at 1x80k N=8 the step is 120.5 ms, FA 29 ms, kv_convert 8 ms; the 29 ms is about 9x the single-read floor and 1.5x the six-head re-read floor. Expected gain: if the arbiter shows the 6x re-read, FA 29 ms to about 5-10 ms at 80k (-8..-20% of the step), nil at <= 8k (inferred; TensorFold's L2 is 24 MB, ours 8 MB).
**Arbiter first (needs a GPU grant, no build):** rocprof the champion at 1x80k N=8 recording per FA dispatch `FETCH_SIZE` and `TCC_HIT`/`TCC_MISS`. `FETCH_SIZE` >= 3x the f16 K+V view bytes means the re-read is real. About 1x means L2 already absorbs it and this seed is dead.
**Falsifier:** `TCC_HIT` rate on FA dispatches rises by < 20 points, or FA time drops by less than the window noise floor at 1x80k. Correctness: byte-identical on `test-backend-ops -o FLASH_ATTN_EXT` (D=256, GQA 6, q8_0, padded and unpadded), the DF2-9 reproducer, greedy identity, the RI gate. Effort S.

## akg-fa-q8-kv-tile-widen-no-convert (proposed) -- widen q8_0 K/V tiles inside FA instead of converting the whole view (27B GPU, bit-exact by construction, rank 2)
Mechanism: delete the whole-view q8_0 to f16 conversion before multi-row FA (`fattn-common.cuh:1022-1041`). Widen each K/V tile in the kernel as it is staged into LDS with the same op sequence the convert kernel uses (`float(d)*q`, then f16 round), so LDS values are bit-identical. TensorFold #1168 / #1080 `F8` staging. Reuse `fattn-vec`'s quantised-K dequant helpers; the WMMA/TILE loaders need a q8_0 staging path. Expected gain: -8 ms at 80k from deleting kv_convert (-6.6% of the step, +7% tok/s), and FA K/V reads fall from 2.0 to about 1.06 B per element (up to -45% of FA bytes if byte-bound). Claim nothing for prefill (under 1% by arithmetic).
**Falsifier:** kv_convert at 1x80k is < 2 ms on the champion, or the in-kernel widen costs more VALU than it saves (FA time rises by more than the convert time removed). Correctness: same suite as the grid-order seed plus a q8_0 tile edge at n_kv not a multiple of the tile; the DF2-9 gate is mandatory (rocWMMA FA NaN class, LDS pressure at D=256). Effort M.

## akg-fa-gqa-rows-packed-mfma-tile (proposed) -- pack the 6 GQA heads times N window rows into one MFMA tile (27B GPU, tolerance-class, rank 3, after the two above have measured)
Mechanism: put the 6 query heads of a KV head times the N window rows (48 lines at N=8) into the M dimension of one MFMA tile (3 x m16), so each q8_0 K/V tile is read once per KV head per round, staged and widened in LDS (the widen seed's loader). TensorFold #1168 (`attn_one`/`attn_rows`). The CUDA MMA kernel already packs GQA (`ncols2`, `fattn.cu:113-150`), so the port is `ncols2` packing on the gfx90a MFMA path at D=256. Expected gain: FA bytes fall to one q8 read (2.85 GB, 1.7 ms floor at 80k); with the widen seed, up to -20..-28% of the 80k step as a ceiling.
**Falsifier:** after the two cheaper seeds land, FA at 1x80k is within 1.5x of its q8 single-read floor (nothing left), or the packed tile needs > 64 KiB LDS at D=256 and occupancy collapses. It changes the reduction order, so it is not bit-exact: it must be row-invariant (each row identical alone and in any N), pass the ppl/KLD contract, the DF2-9 reproducer and within-candidate greedy identity across N. Author separately switchable (KVU-19b precedent). Effort L.

## akg-dflash2-drafter-vocab-span-head (proposed) -- compute DFlash2 drafter logits only over the vocabulary spans drafts can come from (27B GPU, output-neutral, low)
Mechanism: restrict the drafter head to the spans its drafts can draw from, falling back to the full head once a stream holds a token outside them. Verification decides every token, so target output is unchanged. TensorFold `e475e90fb9`: head 450 to 182 us per round, round -1.2%. Expected gain: <= 1% of the 75.8 ms round by byte share.
**Falsifier:** `dflash.cpp` already projects only the drafter vocabulary (d2t), or the head is under 1% of the round in rocprof. Risk: acceptance loss on multilingual if the fallback is wrong. Effort S-M.

## akm-q38fn-cpy-producer-writes-consumer-layout (proposed) -- producers write the layout and type their matmul consumer reads (Q38FN CPU, bit-exact)
Mechanism: copies are exact, so a producer that emits the consumer's layout removes a CPY node, its barrier and its memory round-trip (TensorFold `b9b23ec9ca`, `11e9b4db18`, `e72a34ae38`). Step 1, no build: classify the CPY nodes in a `--node-profile` of the champion at N=5 by source op and destination consumer (permute to contiguous before `MUL_MAT`, f32 to f16/bf16 casts, conv-state or KV writes). Step 2: for the largest class whose producer is a graph op in `src/models/qwen4exp*.cpp`, have the producer emit the target tensor, or let the consumer read the strided view. Measured census (note 62): N=5 verify eval wall 77.7 ms, 4,480 nodes, CPY 9.4% of wall (7.3 ms); about 1,800 small nodes at about 7.7 us. Expected gain: realistic 2-4% after the norm-to-q8 KEEP takes its share (ceiling 9.4%). Q38FN is served on the GPU, so this is the CPU-lane variant.
**Falsifier:** after norm-to-q8-fusion CPY is under 3% of wall, or the CPY classes are KV `set_rows` / state writes that must exist. Bit-exact: greedy identity and node-output byte equality. Shared-op edits need the C112 peer check. Effort S-M.

## akm-q38fn-gdn-prologue-into-chain (proposed) -- fold the GDN prologue and gated-norm epilogue into GATED_DELTA_NET (Q38FN CPU, bit-exact)
Mechanism: fold the conv tail, q/k/v/g/beta preparation and l2norm, plus the gated-norm epilogue, into the `GATED_DELTA_NET` op row by row (TensorFold #1217 `d0d179497e`, -19% of the GDN block; `eb0d391600` skips the redundant conv commit on a one-window verify round). The CPU GDN op already writes rollback snapshots in-kernel (`ggml/src/ggml-cpu/ops.cpp:11134-11154`), so only the prologue/epilogue node count applies. Expected gain: GDN is 8.3% of wall; about 5-10 nodes per GDN layer at about 7.7 us is 1-3% of wall if the fused graph has not already removed them.
**Falsifier:** `qwen4exp-fused.cpp` (`akm-cpu-qwen4exp-fused-decode-nle8` lineage) already merges the GDN prologue, or the prologue nodes are under 1% of wall in the node profile. Run this before authoring. Shared `ggml/` op: shape-gate it and peer-check (C112). Overlaps `akm-cpu-small-node-prelude-collapse`. Effort M.

## akm-ds41-1017-dsa-indexer-fused-scan (proposed, RECORD-ONLY) -- one-pass lightning-indexer score scan (DS41 CPU)
Mechanism: score the indexer in one pass over the compressed index-key cache: per key accumulate `d[h] = q_h . k` with the query held in registers/L1, then `s = sum_h w_h * relu(d[h]*scale)` in the same loop, writing one score per key; never materialise the `[index heads x keys]` tensor. Keep the reference head and dimension order so scores and `top_k` ties are bit-identical. TensorFold `aa6fbd8903` (#1054): 16,384 keys x 16 rows 1.59 to 0.14 ms (author's Mac, scores bit-equal). Not measurable on the current DS41 target (N=1 decode, 45-token prompt); it matters only at 32k/64k with the AKX-ALL-12 attention-graph route and a long-context surface. DS41 is not served, so this seed is a record, not a build request.
**Falsifier:** at 32k/64k the indexer nodes (matmul, relu, weighting, head-sum) are under 2% of decode wall in an at-depth `--node-profile`. Effort M.
