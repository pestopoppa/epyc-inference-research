## AK-Q8WV-OVERLAP-REMAINING (proposed, `design_prior`) -- partial q8wv overlap only if it beats the touch result
Two designs are dead (full second set: 128 VGPRs with scratch; next-step touch: about 5% slower). Inferred, not measured: the addressable limit is at most 1.05-1.10x of q8wv kernel time at d8192, and the composed near-keep route to 1.10x is gone (`/mnt/raid0/llm/tmp/near-keep-census-20261109/`, arithmetic only). Untried: a partial prefetch (YT=2) or fewer waves; both reduce the overlap or concurrency on an already marginal estimate.
Keep rule (from the closed box): np4 DFlash2@d32768 >= 1.05x C9, v9 verify batch >= 1.10x at d8192, no floor regression, output identity.
**Falsifier:** first three pairs < 1.03x, or any shape above 128 VGPRs or with scratch. Effort M. Priority low.

## AK-CPU-MOE-BEATS-REPACK (proposed, `design_prior`) -- MoE kernels must beat CPU_REPACK
Inferred from the measured IQ4_NL null: Q38FN experts already use AVX2 `iq4_nl_8x8_q8_0` repack, and DS41-1017 MXFP4 experts have the same shape. Any MoE quant kernel must first win against the repack path on the op-level census, then e2e. A win against native per-row `vec_dot` proves nothing.
**Falsifier:** op-level repack/new < 1.05x on the real expert shape census. Effort M.
