# Seeds from the MI210 served-path bandwidth census (2026-11-22)

Source: epyc-root `docs/design/mi210-served-kernel-bandwidth-census-20261122.md`, commits 62a1b9538 and 1cd2e55dc.
Operator rule (2026-11-22): a served GPU kernel below **1 TB/s** effective is an open surface. The stream ceiling is
about 1.5 TB/s and the target is 1.2 TB/s. Champion is C18 `74c18e2d8`.

Grade: `design_prior`. Op numbers come from existing rows on C17-era binaries, not from C18. Gate class: futility-only.
Every seed below has recoverable ≥0.3 ms/round.

Cross-check against the existing seeds:
- The A3B router (MMVF cap) is already KEEP in C16; see seed 13. It is NOT re-seeded, only listed as residual.
- MMID 4297a2caa has its own pending row, `ak-mmid-mf-gpu-20261122`. BW-MMID below annotates it and does not
  duplicate it.
- Seed 19 (Q8MF reuse) covers the prefill tail, not M=8..16 verify. BW-DENSE-M16 is distinct.

## BW-DENSE-M16 (proposed) -- 27B V5I dense Q8_0 at verify M=8..16 to ≥1.15 TB/s
Kernel: V5I Q8_MF dense GEMV/GEMM (`GGML_HIP_Q8_MF=6`, INPLACE, `WVSK_MAX=16`). Measured on row
`ak-q8wv-split16-gpu-20261122-r3` arm V:
- 17408x5120 (ffn gate/up): 1047 GB/s at M=8, 1018 at M=16.
- 5120x17408 (ffn down): 1067 / 1017.
- 6144-class shapes (attn_gate, ssm_out, attn_output, 33 MB): **906-976 GB/s, below the floor**.
Dense total is 25.7 GB per forward in 24.7 ms at M=8 (1.04 TB/s).
**Idea:** split-K, or more workgroups per row, for the ≤34 MB shapes so the tail CUs do not idle; one fused
gate+up launch (189 MB) to amortise ramp-up and drain.
**Expected:** 3.3 ms/round at M=8 and 4.4 at M=16, which is 7-10% of the 45.7 ms DF2 round.
**Covered by:** V5I is the base. Split16 failed correctness (`ak-q8wv-split16-gpu-20261122-r3` FAIL_CORRECTNESS) and V5I supersedes it. Gate+up fusion is new.
**Stop rules:** op-level test-backend-ops cells in the r3 format, ≥5 interleaved reps vs C18 V5I. Keep if the
geomean over 6144-class and ffn cells at M 8..16 is ≥1.08 and no cell is <0.98. Stop-on-futility on any correctness
failure.
**Falsifier:** the best split-K variant of the 6144-class shapes stays <1.0 TB/s at M=8, or the gain is <3% op time.

## BW-A3B-DENSE (proposed) -- A3B dense MMVQ apparent 224 GB/s at MTP verify
Kernel: `mul_mat_vec_q<Q8_0, ncols>` dense on A3B (attention, GDN projections, shared experts, lm_head). On the C14 n=4
trace (`rka-a3b-n4-trace-gpu-20261118`) it took 9.1 ms/step for about 2.04 GB of weights, an apparent 224 GB/s. The
attribution is unresolved: 620 calls/step exceeds 251 tensors, so the trace window likely includes MTP draft passes.
**Step 1 (measurement only):** rocprofv3 trace of C18 A3B MTP n=3 in llama-server, split by dispatch grid into draft and verify,
V5I ON and OFF arms. Pending row (not yet scaffolded): `ak-c18-a3b-mtp-trace-gpu`.
**Step 2 (only if step 1 confirms <600 GB/s on verify):** route the n=3 dense matmuls through V5I/q8wv, or widen the MMVQ
rows-per-block for k=2048.
**Expected:** up to 7 ms/step (≤20%) if the 224 GB/s holds. Likely much less.
**Covered by:** V5I partly; its A3B e2e of 0.988 is mostly noise (root `v5i-a3b-0988-diagnosis-20261122.md`).
**Falsifier:** verify-only dense MMVQ in the C18 trace is ≥900 GB/s. Close.

## BW-GDN-RESIDENT (proposed) -- GDN recurrence across M verify rows with state on-chip
Kernel: `gated_delta_net_cuda<128,...>`, 48 layers on the 27B. It takes 1.46 ms/fwd at M=8 and 2.70 at M=16
(`ak-27b-verify-slope-attr-gpu-20261122`), slope 0.155 ms/row. Approximate state traffic: about 6 MB/layer read+write,
so about 200 GB/s effective.
**Idea:** keep each head's 128x128 state in registers/LDS across all M tokens. Write only the snapshots the DF2
rollback/RS_REPLAY needs.
**Expected:** 1.2 ms/round at M=8, 2.4 at M=16 (3-5%).
**Covered by:** none. FAROWS-INPLACE and H12 do not touch GDN.
**Stop rules:** first a byte check from the state size and the RS_REPLAY mode. Then op gate: ≥1.3x on GDN op time at M 8/16
with bit-exact output vs C18.
**Falsifier:** the byte check shows the kernel already moves ≥0.8 TB/s, because the snapshot writes dominate. Close.

## BW-MMID (annotation, not a new seed) -- A3B MUL_MAT_ID to ≥1.0 TB/s at n 3..16
Kernel: `mul_mat_vec_q_moe<Q8_0,2>`. Row `ak-mmid-split16-gpu-20261121-r4`: gate_up 571 GB/s at n=1, 814 at n=3,
849 at n=4; down 637 / 750 / 752. Occupancy U(3)≈23 experts ≈3.08 GB in 3.95 ms ≈780 GB/s.
**Expected:** 1.4-1.6 ms/step (4-5%) at 1.2 TB/s.
**Covered by:** **4297a2caa**, row `ak-mmid-mf-gpu-20261122` (pending). Do not propose a parallel MMID kernel until
that verdict lands.
**Falsifier:** 4297a2caa reaches ≥1.0 TB/s at n≥4. Then this surface is closed below n=16.

## BW-ROUTER-RESIDUAL (annotation) -- A3B router after the MMVF cap knob
On C14 the router took 4.6 ms/step (13.4%), about 18 GB/s, launch/shape-bound. The MMVF cap (`79650eeab`, seed 13) has since landed.
**Falsifier:** the C18 trace (BW-A3B-DENSE step 1) shows router plus topk chain <1 ms/step. Then it is closed.
